History of Sigeonssenpexia

Sigeonssenpexia was an ancient country inhabited primarily by three major groups, the Sigeons, the Sennises, and the Pexes. It existed as a unified state for centuries before political disagreements eventually caused its collapse.

The division of Sigeonssenpexia created the modern countries of Goonland and Sentexland and laid the foundation for the later Goonkrieg.

Early history

Sigeonssenpexia occupied a large territory that eventually became modern-day Goonland and Sentexland.

The country had three major populations.

The Sigeons lived primarily in the western regions. They were known for their social culture, large gatherings, music, and relatively relaxed approach to government.

The Sennises lived primarily in the eastern regions. Their society placed greater importance on organization, military strength, and centralized government.

The Pexes lived throughout the country and became heavily involved in trade and politics.

Despite their differences, the three groups lived together under a single government for centuries.

The country became an important center of trade, with mangoes becoming one of its most valuable commodities.

Political tensions

Relations between the three groups began deteriorating during the final decades of Sigeonssenpexia.

The Sigeons and Sennises increasingly disagreed over taxation, military spending, trade regulations, and control over mango production.

The Pexes attempted to mediate between the two sides, but their efforts failed to stop the growing political divide.

By 2021, the central government had lost control over several regions. The western and eastern governments began operating independently.

Sigeonssenpexia was effectively divided before the split became official.

The Partition

In 2022, representatives from the Sigeons and Sennises met to negotiate the future of the country.

The negotiations lasted several months. The two sides eventually agreed to divide Sigeonssenpexia into two independent states.

The western territory became Goonland, controlled primarily by the Sigeons.

The eastern territory became Sentexland, controlled primarily by the Sennises.

The Pex population was divided between the two countries.

The partition was intended to prevent a civil war. Instead, it created a heavily disputed border.

Goonland

Goonland became the western successor state of Sigeonssenpexia.

The Sigeons established their capital in Goon Central.

The new government adopted a decentralized political system and allowed local communities significant independence.

Goonland quickly developed a reputation for nightlife, music, large gatherings, and an unusually relaxed attitude toward public regulations.

The country also became one of the world's largest consumers of mangoes.

Sentexland

Sentexland became the eastern successor state.

The Sennises established a centralized government and invested heavily in border defenses.

The Sentex government viewed Goonland as politically unstable and feared that the Sigeons would eventually attempt to reclaim eastern territory.

Relations between the two countries deteriorated rapidly.

The Mango Dispute

The final major dispute between Goonland and Sentexland concerned a shipment of 67 mangoes.

The shipment was transported toward Goonland shortly after the partition. It disappeared near the border.

Sentexland accused Goonland of stealing the mangoes. Goonland denied the accusation.

"The mangoes were never stolen. They were relocated."
— Goonland diplomatic statement, 2022

The dispute became increasingly hostile. Negotiations failed, the border was closed, and military forces from both countries began mobilizing.

The Goonkrieg

On 14 June 2022, Sentexland forces crossed into Goonland. The Goonkrieg began.

The war was fought primarily between the Sigeongoons of Goonland and the Sennisgoons of Sentexland.

The conflict lasted until 2025 and became the largest war in the history of the two countries.

The Battle of Rooftop 67

The most famous engagement was the Battle of Rooftop 67, where Sigeongoons forces successfully defended a major mango storage facility.

The battle became a symbol of Goonland's resistance and was later commemorated during the annual Mango 67 celebrations.

Peace

After several years of fighting, both countries faced severe economic problems.

Negotiations resumed in 2025.

The Treaty of Mango 67 ended the Goonkrieg and restored diplomatic relations between Goonland and Sentexland.

The two countries remained independent.

The original 67 mangoes were never found.

Legacy

The dissolution of Sigeonssenpexia remains one of the defining events in the history of the region.

The country's collapse created the modern political division between the Sigeons and Sennises.

The western half became Goonland. The eastern half became Sentexland.

The rivalry between the two countries eventually produced the Goonkrieg, while the disputed mango shipment became one of the most famous political controversies in modern pigeon history.

Today, the former territory of Sigeonssenpexia is divided by the Goonland-Sentexland border.

The name Sigeonssenpexia survives in historical records, old maps, and arguments between historians over who was responsible for losing the 67 mangoes.
