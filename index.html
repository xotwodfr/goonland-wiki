Sigeonssenpexia was a former country inhabited primarily by three major groups, the Sigeons, the Sennises, and the Pexes. It existed as a unified state for centuries before political disagreements between the groups eventually caused its collapse.

The country was divided in 2022, creating the modern states of Goonland in the west and Sentexland in the east. The division eventually led to the Goonkrieg, one of the largest conflicts in modern pigeon history.

The history of Sigeonssenpexia is also closely associated with Sigeon Pex, a prominent Sigeon who later became one of Goonland's most famous social figures.

Early history

Sigeonssenpexia occupied the territory that would later become Goonland and Sentexland. The country's exact date of establishment is unknown, with surviving historical records suggesting that it existed for several centuries.

The country developed into a major center of trade. Its location allowed merchants to travel between the western and eastern regions, while mangoes became one of its most important commodities.

For much of its history, Sigeonssenpexia remained politically unified despite significant cultural differences between its populations.

The three groups

The Sigeons

The Sigeons primarily inhabited the western regions of Sigeonssenpexia. Their society was known for large gatherings, music, social events, and a relatively relaxed approach to government.

The Sigeons later became the dominant population of Goonland.

The Sennises

The Sennises primarily inhabited the eastern regions. Their society placed greater importance on centralized government, organization, military strength, and regulation of trade.

The Sennises later became the dominant population of Sentexland.

The Pexes

The Pexes lived throughout Sigeonssenpexia and became heavily involved in trade and politics.

The Pexes frequently acted as intermediaries between the Sigeons and Sennises.

Political tensions

Relations between the three groups deteriorated during the final decades of Sigeonssenpexia.

The Sigeons and Sennises increasingly disagreed over taxation, military spending, trade regulations, territorial administration, and control over mango production.

The Pexes attempted to mediate between the two sides, but their efforts failed to prevent the political divide from widening.

By 2021, the central government had lost effective control over several regions. Western and eastern authorities began operating independently.

Sigeonssenpexia was effectively divided before the formal partition took place.

The Partition

In 2022, representatives of the Sigeons and Sennises began negotiations over the future of Sigeonssenpexia.

The negotiations lasted several months. Both sides eventually agreed to divide the country into two independent states.

The western territory became Goonland, controlled primarily by the Sigeons.

The eastern territory became Sentexland, controlled primarily by the Sennises.

The Pex population was divided between the two new countries.

The partition was intended to prevent a civil war. Instead, the newly established border became the source of further disputes.

Goonland

Goonland became the western successor state of Sigeonssenpexia.

The Sigeons established their political center in Goon Central.

The new government adopted a relatively decentralized political system and allowed local communities considerable independence.

Goonland quickly became known for nightlife, music, rooftop gatherings, and large social events.

Mangoes became an important part of Goonland's culture and economy.

Sentexland

Sentexland became the eastern successor state of Sigeonssenpexia.

The Sennises established a centralized government and invested heavily in border defenses.

The Sentex government viewed Goonland as politically unstable and feared that the Sigeons would eventually attempt to reclaim eastern territory.

Diplomatic relations between the two countries deteriorated rapidly.

The Mango Dispute

The final major dispute before the Goonkrieg concerned a shipment of 67 mangoes.

The shipment was transported toward Goonland shortly after the partition. It disappeared near the border.

Sentexland accused Goonland of stealing the mangoes. Goonland denied the accusation.

"The mangoes were never stolen. They were relocated."
— Goonland diplomatic statement, 2022

The dispute escalated rapidly. Negotiations failed, the border was closed, and military forces from both countries began mobilizing.

Goonkrieg

On 14 June 2022, Sentexland forces crossed into Goonland. The Goonkrieg began.

The war was fought primarily between the Sigeongoons of Goonland and the Sennisgoons of Sentexland.

The conflict continued until 2025 and became the largest war in the modern history of the two countries.

Battle of Rooftop 67

The Battle of Rooftop 67 became the most famous engagement of the Goonkrieg.

Rooftop 67 overlooked central Goonland and contained a major mango storage facility. Sennisgoon forces attempted to capture the location, while Sigeongoons forces mounted a determined defense.

The battle lasted approximately nine hours. The Sigeongoons eventually launched a counterattack and forced the Sennisgoons from the rooftop.

The victory became a symbol of Goonland's resistance and later inspired the name of the Mango 67 celebrations.

Battle of Goon Central

In 2024, Sentexland launched its largest offensive of the war. Its objective was Goon Central, the largest city in Goonland.

The fighting centered around Goonland Station. The battle lasted throughout the day and involved thousands of pigeons.

Sigeongoons forces eventually received reinforcements and pushed the Sennisgoons out of the city.

The defeat became a major turning point in the war.

End of the war

By early 2025, Sentexland was suffering from severe economic problems and declining morale.

Sigeongoons forces advanced toward the Sentex capital. Sennis eventually agreed to negotiate.

The war ended later in 2025 with the signing of the Treaty of Mango 67.

Sigeon Pex

Sigeon Pex is a prominent Sigeon figure known for his rise from homelessness to becoming one of Goonland's wealthiest and most recognizable social figures.

He is particularly known for hosting the Mango 67 parties at his mansion in Goonland.

Early life

Sigeon Pex was born in a small, overcrowded pigeon nest on the outskirts of Goonland.

His childhood was reportedly difficult. His parents considered him an embarrassment to the family because of his unusual personality, questionable decision-making, and habit of collecting discarded mango peels.

At the age of seven pigeon years, Sigeon was kicked out of the family nest.

Following his expulsion, Sigeon had no permanent home and was forced to survive on the streets of Goonland.

Homeless period

During his homeless period, Sigeon slept underneath benches, abandoned rooftops, and occasionally inside a broken shopping cart.

He survived primarily on crumbs, discarded fries, and whatever mango-related food he found around Goonland.

Despite his circumstances, Sigeon remained determined to improve his life.

His time without a home later became an important part of his public image. After becoming wealthy, he reportedly kept the original broken shopping cart from his homeless period inside his mansion.

Rise to prominence

Sigeon's fortunes began to change after he befriended several pigeons near Goonland Central Station.

Among his earliest known friends were Gregor the Pigeon, known for wearing a tiny red hat, and Pexington, a pigeon who claimed to have "connections everywhere."

The group explored different parts of Goonland together. Sigeon gradually gained a reputation for being unusually friendly and for somehow obtaining large quantities of mangoes.

His growing social circle led to invitations to increasingly exclusive gatherings.

Sigeon became known for organizing events with loud music, enormous quantities of mangoes, and little regard for noise regulations.

His popularity increased steadily.

The mansion

Following the success of his events, Sigeon accumulated considerable wealth.

He eventually purchased a large mansion overlooking Goonland. The property contains several bedrooms, a rooftop terrace, a swimming pool, a large mango storage room, and a dedicated party hall.

The mansion became the unofficial headquarters of the Mango 67 movement.

Sigeon reportedly kept the broken shopping cart from his homeless years inside the mansion as a reminder of his earlier life.

Mango 67

In 2024, Sigeon Pex hosted the first major Mango 67 party at an abandoned warehouse in central Goonland.

The event was initially expected to attract around 20 pigeons. More than 300 reportedly attended.

The exact meaning of the number 67 remains disputed. Some accounts claim it refers to the original number of mangoes purchased for the first party. Other accounts state that Sigeon chose the number because he thought it sounded good.

The parties eventually became a major part of Goonland's nightlife.

Typical Mango 67 parties feature hundreds of pigeons, large quantities of mangoes, loud music, rooftop dancing, and at least one pigeon getting stuck inside a ventilation system.

Sigeon remains the permanent host and is known for personally welcoming guests at the entrance.

Peace and aftermath

Following several years of fighting, both Goonland and Sentexland faced severe economic problems.

Negotiations resumed in 2025.

The Treaty of Mango 67 restored diplomatic relations between Goonland and Sentexland.

Both countries remained independent.

The original 67 mangoes were never recovered.

Former Sigeongoons and Sennisgoons eventually began attending the same social events. Mango 67 parties became an unofficial symbol of reconciliation between the two populations.

Legacy

The dissolution of Sigeonssenpexia remains one of the defining events in the history of the region.

Its collapse created the modern political division between the Sigeons and Sennises.

The western half became Goonland. The eastern half became Sentexland.

The rivalry between the two countries eventually produced the Goonkrieg, while the disputed shipment of 67 mangoes became one of the most famous political controversies in modern pigeon history.

Sigeon Pex's life also became a major part of modern Goonland culture. His rise from homelessness to wealth and his role as host of Mango 67 became one of the most widely known success stories in Goonland.
